# How does it work with other mods?

## Mods built on it

**Valchitecture** uses Chestworks for building. You build with materials from your
chests, and materials from pieces you remove go back into them. Valchitecture's unlocks
decide how far it reaches.

## AzuCraftyBoxes

AzuCraftyBoxes also takes from nearby chests. While it's installed, Chestworks lets
it do that job, so nothing is counted or taken twice. Putting items back into your
chests still works.

## MultiUserChest

MultiUserChest lets several players use a chest at once. With both mods installed and
Chestworks on the server, a chest someone else has open stays usable. Otherwise, a chest
anyone has open is skipped.

## SeneaL UI

SeneaL UI has its own build and crafting panels. Chestworks adds the items in your chests
to their counts and to their "from chests" marker.

If you turn on SeneaL UI's own build-from-chests or craft-from-chests setting, SeneaL UI
takes from chests itself, and Chestworks leaves that part to it.

## Backpack mods

Backpacks and other containers you carry are part of your inventory, so Chestworks
never uses them.
